*   **Visual Strategy**: A chronological, high-density listing of annual compliance reports.

### 4. Report Detail View

### 5. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Precision discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year.

### 6. Compliance Note
*   **Visual Strategy**: A technical "Governance Standards" module detailing J pan's adherence to the Companies Act and SEBI mandates.
