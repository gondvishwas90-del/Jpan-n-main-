# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Material Documents & Contracts" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Material Documents & Contracts" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Material Documents & Contracts.md`. The design will prioritize legal transparency, high-fidelity document management, and professional discoverability.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist legal-industrial imagery (leather-bound ledgers, architectural blueprints, or clean corporate glass).
*   **Message**: "Material Documents & Contracts: Authoritative Disclosures."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography outlining our commitment to statutory transparency and the accessibility of key corporate agreements.

### 3. Discovery Engine (Filters & Search)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A robust, sticky archive discovery bar featuring multi-category filters (Contracts/Agreements/Policies) and a high-precision search utility.

### 4. Institutional Repository (Listing)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density grid or table layout featuring document metadata (Type, Date, Reference) and dual-action triggers (View/Download).

### 5. Immersive Document Hub (Detail Viewer)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A full-screen reading experience with an integrated PDF viewer, document summary, and side-pane for metadata.

### 6. Strategic Highlights
*   **Visual Strategy**: A featured section using elevated card designs for 2-3 critical agreements, providing immediate context for stakeholders.

### 7. Compliance Framework
*   **Visual Strategy**: A minimalist legal disclosure section citing regulatory mandates (SEBI LODR / Companies Act).
