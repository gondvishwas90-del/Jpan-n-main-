Job Detail Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Job title (example: “Production Engineer”)
Location
Experience required
Background: workplace / team image

2. Job Summary
3–4 lines:
Role overview
Department
Reporting to

3. Key Responsibilities (Main 🔥)
Bullet points:
Daily tasks
Role duties
Team collaboration

4. Required Skills & Qualifications
Education
Technical skills
Experience
Certifications (if any)

5. Job Details Info Block
Structured info:
Job type (Full-time)
Location
Experience (years)
Salary (optional)

6. Benefits (Optional)
Points:
Salary benefits
Learning opportunities
Work environment

7. Apply Now Section (Very Important 🔥)
Form fields:
Name
Email
Phone
Resume upload
Message (optional)
Submit button

8. Related Jobs (Optional)
2–3 job cards
“View More Jobs” CTA

9. CTA Section
Line:
“Take the next step in your career”
Button:
Apply Now

10. Footer
Same global footer
