# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "SEBI Disclosures" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "SEBI Disclosures" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `SEBI Disclosures.md`. The design will focus on regulatory precision, institutional integrity, and high-speed compliance discovery.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist, high-contrast imagery (abstract legal textures, corporate pillars, or financial blueprint accents).
*   **Message**: "SEBI Disclosures: Unwavering Commitment to Regulatory Integrity."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography framing SEBI compliance as the bedrock of J pan's market accountability.

### 3. Disclosures Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A systematic, high-density listing of statutory filings.
*   **Row Design**: Clear regulation markers (Reg 30, Reg 46), date stamps, and primary action triggers (View/Download).

### 4. Disclosure Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a professional PDF viewer optimized for official regulatory filings.

### 5. Categories / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Advanced discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year and specific SEBI Regulation types (Reg 30, 33, 44, 46).

### 6. Compliance Statement
*   **Visual Strategy**: A technical "Verified Compliance" module detailing Jpan's adherence to SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional conversion banner with a "Connect with Regulatory Desk" primary action.
