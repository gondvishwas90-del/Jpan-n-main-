# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Press Releases" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Press Releases" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Press Releases.md`. The design will focus on transparency, media authority, and corporate communication.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Clean, corporate backdrop with a "Media Center" feel.
*   **Message**: "Latest News & Official Announcements."

### 2. Featured Press Release
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-impact, full-width or large-card spotlight for the most critical recent update.
*   **Content**: Large imagery, headline, executive summary, and primary "Read More" path.

### 3. Press Releases List
*   **Visual Strategy**: Systematic card-based feed with consistent dates and summaries.
*   **Interactivity**: Hover effects to emphasize readability.

### 4. Archive / Filter Bar
*   **Visual Strategy**: Functional, sticky or top-of-list filter bar.
*   **Features**: Search by keyword, Filter by Year, and Category tabs (Announcements, Products, Financial).

### 5. Media Mentions
*   **Visual Strategy**: Logo-driven "In the News" section featuring external publication credits.
*   **Theme**: External validation and industry leadership.

### 6. Press Release Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist, typography-focused reading experience.
*   **Features**: Image carousels, PDF download options, and "Back to News" navigation.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Subscription-focused invitation to stay informed.
