# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Blog" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Blog" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Blog Articles.md`. The design will focus on thought leadership, technical expertise, and industry education.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist industrial workspace or a high-tech engineering lab shot.
*   **Message**: "Insights, Innovation, & Industry Knowledge."

### 2. Featured Article
*   **Visual Strategy**: Premium large-card spotlight with high-fidelity imagery and a "Thought Leadership" tag.
*   **Content**: Headline, executive summary, and a clear "Continue Reading" path.

### 3. Blog Listing
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-fidelity 3-column grid featuring large imagery and clean typography.
*   **Interactivity**: "Reading Time" indicators and smooth hover transitions.

### 4. Categories & Search
*   **Visual Strategy**: Sticky sub-navigation bar with active states for categories (Technology, Insights, Updates).
*   **Feature**: Integrated real-time search for article discovery.

### 5. Article Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Cinematic, typography-first layout optimized for readability.
*   **Features**: Social share integration, author profiles, and multi-media content support.

### 6. Related Articles
*   **Visual Strategy**: A horizontal "You May Also Like" strip at the end of each article.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Knowledge-driven subscription invitation.
