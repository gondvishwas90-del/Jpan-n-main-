# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Unclaimed & Unpaid Amounts" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Unclaimed & Unpaid Amounts" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Unclaimed & Unpaid.md`. The design will focus on investor transparency, data accessibility, and a streamlined claim recovery process.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-fidelity financial imagery (secure vaults, architectural precision, or frosted glass textures).
*   **Message**: "Unclaimed & Unpaid Amounts: Ensuring Your Financial Interests."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the nature of unclaimed dividends and J pan's commitment to facilitating shareholder claims.

### 3. Search & Filter Engine
*   **Visual Strategy**: A robust, real-time discovery bar allowing shareholders to search for records by Name and filter by Fiscal Year. Includes high-visibility "Quick Claim" triggers.

### 4. Amounts Listing (Table)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-precision, data-dense table layout featuring Shareholder Name, Amount (₹), Year, and Status (Unclaimed/Unpaid).

### 5. Claim Process (Step-by-Step)
*   **Visual Strategy**: An instructional, icon-based roadmap detailing the recovery journey—from document preparation to final claim submission.

### 6. Compliance & IEPF Note
*   **Visual Strategy**: A statutory disclosure module explaining the transfer of funds to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) as per the Companies Act.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: A dedicated "Claim Assistance" conversion banner with direct links to the compliance desk.
