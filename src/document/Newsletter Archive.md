Newsletter Archive Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Newsletter Archive”
Short tagline (example: “Explore our past newsletters and updates”)
Background: corporate / email / office image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
What newsletters include (updates, insights, announcements)
Optional:
Subscribe CTA button

3. Newsletter Listing (Main 🔥)
List / grid layout
Each Newsletter Card:
Newsletter title (Month/Edition name)
Date (Month + Year)
Short 1–2 line summary
“View / Read” button
(Optional) Download PDF button

4. Filter / Archive Navigation
Filter by:
Year
Month
Search bar

5. Newsletter Detail View (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Date
Full newsletter content (or embedded PDF)
Images (if any)
Download option

6. Subscribe Section (Important 🔥)
Line:
“Get our latest updates directly in your inbox”
Email input field
Subscribe button

7. CTA Section
Line:
“Stay connected with our latest developments”
Button:
Contact Us

8. Footer
Same global footer
