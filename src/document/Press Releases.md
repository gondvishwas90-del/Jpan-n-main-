Press Releases Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Press Releases”
Short tagline (example: “Latest news and official announcements”)
Background: corporate / media image

2. Featured Press Release (Optional 🔥)
Highlight latest/important news
Block:
Large image
Title
Short summary
Date
“Read More” CTA

3. Press Releases List (Main)
List / grid layout
Each Card:
Title
Date
Short 2–3 line summary
“Read More” button

4. Press Release Detail Page (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Date
Full article content
Images (if any)
Download PDF (optional)

5. Media Mentions (Optional Premium)
Logos of media platforms
Short mention headline

6. Archive / Filter (Important)
Filter by:
Year
Category (news, announcements, updates)
Search bar

7. CTA Section
Line:
“Stay updated with our latest developments”
Button:
Contact Us / Subscribe

8. Footer
Same global footer
