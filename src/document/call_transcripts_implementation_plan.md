# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Call Transcripts" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Call Transcripts" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Call Transcripts.md`. The design will focus on written transparency, document accessibility, and structured information delivery.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist corporate imagery (structured documents, executive boardrooms, or abstract typographic patterns).
*   **Message**: "Call Transcripts: The Definitive Record of Dialogue."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the role of transcripts as a statutory written record of investor communications.

### 3. Archive Discovery (Filter Bar)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A robust, sticky discovery bar allowing users to filter transcripts by Year, Quarter (Q1-Q4), and Call Type (Earnings, Investor).

### 4. Transcripts Listing (Grid)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density grid of transcript cards featuring Title, Date, Call Type, and "Read Transcript" triggers.

### 5. Immersive Reading Hub (Modal)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-fidelity document viewer modal designed for extended reading, featuring structured typography (Speaker Tags, Time Markers) and PDF export capabilities.

### 6. Related Recordings Connectivity
*   **Visual Strategy**: A secondary "Acoustic Context" module linking users back to the corresponding Audio Recordings for each transcript.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: A professional communication banner for further investor inquiries.
