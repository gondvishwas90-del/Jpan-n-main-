Credit Rating Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Credit Rating”
Short tagline (example: “Our creditworthiness evaluated by leading rating agencies”)
Background: finance / corporate image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
What credit rating means
Importance for investors & stakeholders

3. Current Credit Rating Highlight (Main 🔥)
Big card / banner
Details:
Rating value (example: “A+ / Stable”)
Rating agency name
Date of rating
Short explanation (what rating indicates)

4. Rating History Listing
Table / list layout
Each Row/Card:
Rating
Agency
Date
Outlook (Stable / Positive / Negative)
“View Report” / “Download PDF”

5. Rating Agency Details (Optional)
Short info about agency
Logo + name

6. Detailed Report View (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Date
Full report (embedded PDF)
Download option

7. Financial Strength Note (Optional)
Short section:
Company stability
Growth performance
Risk profile

8. CTA Section
Line:
“For more financial and investor information, contact us”
Button:
Contact Us

9. Footer
Same global footer
