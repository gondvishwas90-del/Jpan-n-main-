General Meeting Notices Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “General Meeting Notices”
Short tagline (example: “Official notices for shareholder meetings”)
Background: corporate / meeting image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
Purpose of notices
Transparency & shareholder communication

3. Notices Listing (Main 🔥)
Table / list layout
Each Row/Card:
Meeting type:
AGM / EGM
Title (example: “AGM Notice 2025”)
Date of meeting
Short description (1 line)
“View” button
“Download PDF” button

4. Notice Detail View (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Meeting type
Date
Full notice content / embedded PDF
Download option

5. Archive / Filter
Filter by:
Year
Meeting type (AGM / EGM)
Search option

6. Important Instructions (Optional)
Short points:
Participation guidelines
Voting / proxy info

7. CTA Section
Line:
“For any queries regarding meetings, contact us”
Button:
Contact Us

8. Footer
Same global footer
