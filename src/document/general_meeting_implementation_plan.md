# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "General Meeting Notices" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "General Meeting Notices" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `General Meeting.md`. The design will focus on corporate governance, shareholder clarity, and technical accessibility.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-end corporate imagery (close-up of a boardroom or a modern auditorium).
*   **Message**: "General Meeting Notices: Empowering Shareholder Voice & Participation."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography emphasizing the core principles of corporate transparency and democratic participation.

### 3. Notices Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A systematic, high-density listing of AGM and EGM notices.
*   **Row Design**: Clear meeting type indicators (AGM/EGM), date highlights, and primary action triggers (View/Download).

### 4. Notice Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a technical PDF viewer optimized for legal notice text.

### 5. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Dual discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year and meeting category (AGM vs EGM).

### 6. Important Instructions
*   **Visual Strategy**: A dedicated grid of "Instruction Cards" covering voting protocols, proxy submissions, and participation guidelines.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional banner with an "Inquire About Meetings" primary action.
