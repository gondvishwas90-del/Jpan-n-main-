This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Statement of Deviations" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Statement of Deviations.md`. The design will focus on fiscal transparency, regulatory precision, and high-speed disclosure discovery.

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist, high-contrast financial imagery (abstract data textures, corporate ledgers, or high-fidelity architectural pillars).
*   **Message**: "Statement of Deviations: Transparency in Fiscal Adherence."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography framing the statement of deviations as a cornerstone of J pan's financial integrity and fund utilization accountability.

### 3. Statements Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A periodic, high-density listing of statutory statements.
*   **Row Design**: Clear fiscal period markers (Quarter/Year), date stamps, and primary action triggers (View/Download).

### 4. Statement Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a professional PDF viewer optimized for official statements of deviations.

### 5. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Advanced discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year and quarterly cycles.

### 6. Compliance Note
*   **Visual Strategy**: A technical "Regulatory Disclosure" module detailing J pan's adherence to SEBI norms regarding fund utilization and deviations.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional conversion banner with a "Connect with Financial Disclosure Desk" primary action.
