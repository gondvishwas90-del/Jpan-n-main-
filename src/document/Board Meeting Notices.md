Board Meeting Notices Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Board Meeting Notices”
Short tagline (example: “Official notices for board meetings and decisions”)
Background: corporate meeting / boardroom image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
Purpose of board meeting notices
Governance & transparency statement

3. Notices Listing (Main 🔥)
Table / list layout
Each Row/Card:
Meeting title (example: “Board Meeting – Q1 2025”)
Date of meeting
Short description (agenda highlight – 1 line)
“View” button
“Download PDF” button

4. Notice Detail View (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Meeting date
Agenda summary
Full notice content / embedded PDF
Download option

5. Archive / Filter
Filter by:
Year
Quarter (optional)
Search option

6. Governance Note (Optional)
Short section:
Board structure
Compliance practices

7. CTA Section
Line:
“For more governance-related information, contact us”
Button:
Contact Us

8. Footer
Same global footer
