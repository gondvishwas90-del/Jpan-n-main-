# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Investor Presentation" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Investor Presentation" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Investor Presentation.md`. The design will focus on visual storytelling, strategic growth narratives, and high-fidelity financial reporting.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Analytics-themed imagery (3D charts, high-tech financial interfaces, or abstract growth curves).
*   **Message**: "Investor Presentation: Strategic Insights & Performance Growth."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography framing presentations as a roadmap of J pan's operational excellence and future trajectory.

### 3. Presentations Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A grid-based "Card" layout. Each card represents a strategic interaction (Quarterly Results, Capital Markets Day, etc.).
*   **Card Design**: High-contrast typography, date stamps, and primary action triggers (View/Download).

### 4. Presentation Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a professional slide viewer optimized for technical business presentations.

### 5. Key Highlights (Premium)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-impact metrics dashboard showing Revenue Growth, Market Presence, and Expansion Plans with animated counters and industrial icons.

### 6. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Advanced discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year and quarterly cycle (Q1–Q4).

### 7. Investor Communication CTA
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional conversion banner with a "Connect with Investor Relations" primary action.
