# BRAIN.md

## J PAN TUBULAR COMPONENTS LIMITED

### Digital Experience Design Brain

---

# 01 — CORE IDEA

The J Pan website is not a conventional manufacturing website.

It is a digital representation of:

> **Precision Engineering.**

Every visual, interaction, transition and piece of content must reinforce this idea.

The website should make visitors immediately understand:

* What J Pan manufactures
* Where its products are used
* Why its engineering capability matters
* How precise its manufacturing is
* Why OEMs can trust the company
* How to contact the company

---

# 02 — BRAND EXPERIENCE

The experience should feel:

**Precise**

**Industrial**

**Technical**

**Premium**

**Reliable**

**Global**

**Intelligent**

Never make the website feel:

* Cheap
* Generic
* Template-based
* Overly corporate
* Sci-fi
* Artificially futuristic
* Over-animated

---

# 03 — DESIGN QUALITY BAR

The visual quality target is:

### Apple-level

For:

* spacing
* typography
* product presentation
* simplicity
* visual hierarchy

### Stripe-level

For:

* information architecture
* technical storytelling
* interaction design
* structured data presentation

### Rive-level

For:

* interactive graphics
* state-based animation
* responsive motion
* lightweight interactive experiences

### Awwwards-level

For:

* visual craft
* transitions
* storytelling
* memorable interactions

These are quality references, NOT templates to copy.

# 07 — PRODUCT-FIRST THINKING

Products are not simply cards.

Products are engineered solutions.

Whenever appropriate, show:

* Material
* Geometry
* Application
* Manufacturing process
* Technical properties
* Quality/testing
* Related products

Use visual storytelling to explain the product.

---

# 08 — 3D MODEL STANDARD

When a 3D product model is requested:

### ACCURACY IS MANDATORY.

Do not create a generic object that only visually resembles the product.

The model must be based on available:

* Product images
* Dimensions
* Technical drawings
* Product specifications
* Reference photographs
* CAD/reference assets

If accurate dimensions are provided, preserve them.

If dimensions are unavailable, do not invent technical dimensions.

The model may be visually reconstructed from references, but it must not be presented as dimensionally verified.

---

# 09 — 3D MODEL QUALITY

Required qualities:

* Correct proportions
* Correct geometry
* Correct bends
* Correct joints
* Correct material appearance
* Correct component relationships
* Clean topology
* Appropriate polygon count
* Realistic manufacturing details

For tubular components, pay particular attention to:

* tube diameter
* wall thickness where provided
* bend radius
* joint geometry
* fittings
* openings
* connection points
* material identity

---

# 10 — 3D PRODUCT PRESENTATION

3D products can be presented using:

* controlled rotation
* exploded views
* specification callouts
* sectional views
* hotspot annotations
* material highlighting
* interactive inspection
* engineering labels

Example:

```text
                 99.9% COPPER
                      ●
                      │
             ┌────────┴───────┐
             │                │
             │   3D PRODUCT   │
             │                │
             └────────────────┘
                      │
                 BEND RADIUS
                      ●
```

Callouts must point to the actual relevant location.

---

# 11 — AI IDENTITY

AI should never dominate the visual identity.

The website should not look like an AI startup.

AI should feel like:

> **Engineering intelligence.**

AI can assist with:

* product discovery
* technical explanation
* product matching
* enquiry preparation
* specification interpretation
* intelligent search

Never invent technical information.

---

# 12 — INTERACTION PHILOSOPHY

Every interaction must have a reason.

Use interaction to:

* explain
* reveal
* compare
* guide
* confirm
* demonstrate

Do not animate an element simply because it can be animated.

---

# 13 — CONVERSION

Primary conversion:

> **Request an Engineering Quote**

Secondary:

> **Explore Products**

Supporting:

> **Talk to an Engineer**

The conversion experience must feel B2B and professional.

---

# 14 — FINAL RULE

If a design element does not improve:

**understanding, credibility, usability or conversion**

remove it.
