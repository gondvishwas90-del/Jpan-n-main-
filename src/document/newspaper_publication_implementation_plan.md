# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Newspaper Publication" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Newspaper Publication" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Newspaper Publication.md`. The design will focus on media transparency, public notice accessibility, and regulatory disclosure precision.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist media imagery (newspaper textures, press silhouettes, or abstract print patterns).
*   **Message**: "Newspaper Publication: Public Notices & Transparency."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the role of newspaper publications as a mandatory statutory requirement for market awareness.

### 3. Archive Discovery (Filter Bar)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A robust, sticky discovery bar allowing users to filter notices by Year, Newspaper Name (e.g., Financial Express, Jansatta), and Language (English/Regional).

### 4. Publications Listing (Grid)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density grid of publication cards featuring Title, Newspaper Name, Publication Date, and Language markers.

### 5. Publication Detail View (Modal)
*   **Visual Strategy**: An immersive viewer modal designed for high-fidelity media clippings, featuring zoom capabilities and document metadata.

### 6. Compliance Note
*   **Visual Strategy**: A statutory disclosure module explaining the legal requirements behind newspaper publications as per SEBI and the Companies Act.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: A professional inquiry banner for public disclosure assistance.
