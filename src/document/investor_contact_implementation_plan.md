# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Investor Contact" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Investor Contact" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Investor Contact.md`. The design will prioritize accessibility, clarity, and institutional trust.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist corporate office imagery (architectural glass, reception lobby, or clean desk setup).
*   **Message**: "Investor Contact: Direct Channels for Transparency."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the importance of seamless investor communication and support.

### 3. Institutional Contact Repository (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-density information blocks for the Investor Relations Officer and the Registrar & Transfer Agent (RTA).

### 4. Inquiry Engine (Form)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A clean, high-precision contact form with real-time validation for specific investor queries.

### 5. Geospatial Hub (Map & Offices)
*   **Visual Strategy**: Integrated Google Map embed and structured office address cards for physical accessibility.

### 6. Support Framework
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist indicators for response timelines and working hours.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: A final supportive reach-out banner for immediate assistance.
