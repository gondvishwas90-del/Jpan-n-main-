Customers Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Our Customers”
Short tagline (example: “Trusted by leading brands across industries”)
Background: industrial / client collaboration image

2. Clients Logos Section (Main 🔥)
Grid / slider of client logos
Each Item:
Company logo
(Optional) industry tag

3. Key Clients Highlight (Optional Premium)
3–4 featured clients
Each Block:
Logo
Short description of relationship
Industry

4. Industries We Serve (Linked Trust)
Small section:
HVAC
Automotive
Appliances
Icons + short text

5. Testimonials Section
2–4 client reviews
Each Card:
Feedback text
Client name
Company name

6. Case Study Preview (Optional 🔥)
2–3 case study cards
Each Card:
Problem (1 line)
Solution (1 line)
Result (1 line)
“View Details” CTA

7. Long-Term Partnerships Section
Short content:
Years of collaboration
Repeat clients
Trust points:
Reliability
Consistency
Quality

8. CTA Section
Line:
“Join our growing network of satisfied customers”
Button:
Get Quote / Contact Us

Same global footer
