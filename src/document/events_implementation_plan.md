# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Events" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Events" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Events.md`. The design will focus on industry presence, professional networking, and corporate culture.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-energy shot of an industrial exhibition booth or a corporate networking event.
*   **Message**: "Connect with Us Across the Globe."

### 2. Upcoming Events
*   **Visual Strategy**: Clean, calendar-style cards with countdown or date highlights.
*   **Content**: Event Name, Date, Location, and a brief "Why Visit Us" tagline.

### 3. Past Events
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-fidelity grid of event cards with large imagery.
*   **Interactivity**: "View Gallery" triggers for historical event archives.

### 4. Exhibitions & Trade Shows
*   **Visual Strategy**: Logo-driven section highlighting international expos (e.g., HVAC R, Automotive Fairs).
*   **Theme**: Global reach and industry authority.

### 5. Corporate Activities
*   **Visual Strategy**: Warm, team-centric imagery focusing on internal growth and community.
*   **Categories**: Training, Team Events, CSR Activities.

### 6. Achievements & Awards
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist "Trophy" icons with high-contrast text.
*   **Content**: Awards received at trade shows or corporate recognitions.

### 7. Event Detail Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Modal or expanded view for deep-dives into specific major events.
*   **Content**: Multi-image gallery and participation highlights.

### 8. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional invitation to schedule a meeting at an upcoming event.
