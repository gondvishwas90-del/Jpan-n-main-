About Us Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “About Us”
Short tagline (company positioning)
Background: factory / team image

2. Company Overview
6–8 lines intro about company
What company does (manufacturing + industry)
Years of experience highlight

3. Vision & Mission
Two blocks:
Vision (future goal)
Mission (how they work)
Short 2–3 lines each

4. Our Journey / Timeline
Timeline layout (horizontal/vertical)
Key milestones:
Founded year
Expansion
New plants
Major achievements

5. Leadership / Team (Optional but premium)
Founder / directors
Image + name + designation
Short description

6. Manufacturing Strength
Key capabilities:
Machines
Production capacity
Technology
Small supporting images

7. Quality & Certifications
ISO / certifications logos
Short trust statement about quality

8. Our Presence / Locations
Map or list:
Head office
Plants / units

9. Why Choose Us (Short Version)
4–5 points:
Experience
Quality
Reliability
Custom solutions

10. CTA Section
Line:
“Partner with us for reliable manufacturing”
Button:
Contact Us / Get Quote

11. Footer
Same as global footer
