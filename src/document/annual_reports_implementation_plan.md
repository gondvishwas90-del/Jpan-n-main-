# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Annual Reports" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Annual Reports" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Annual Reports.md`. The design will focus on historical depth, clarity of data, and professional accessibility.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-end corporate imagery (close-up of textured paper or a modern glass-facade headquarters).
*   **Message**: "Annual Reports: A Record of Resilience & Strategic Innovation."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography emphasizing transparency and long-term vision.

### 3. Reports Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A robust grid of high-fidelity "Report Cards."
*   **Card Design**: Prominent year display, metallic accents, and dual-action buttons (View/Download).

### 4. Detailed Report View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a technical PDF viewer.
*   **Features**: Full-screen capability, page navigation, and download tracking.

### 5. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Clean, horizontal year-slider or a category-based discovery bar.

### 6. Financial Highlights
*   **Visual Strategy**: Contextual data cards that appear when a specific report is selected or showcased.
*   **Content**: Revenue milestones and key achievement markers for that fiscal year.

### 7. Investor Information
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional disclosure grid consistent with the "Financial Results" page.

### 8. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Strong high-contrast banner with an "Explore More Performance Data" primary action.
