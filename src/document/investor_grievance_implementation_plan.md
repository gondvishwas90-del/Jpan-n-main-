# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Investor Grievance" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Investor Grievance" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Investor Grievance.md`. The design will focus on trust, responsiveness, and professional support.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist support imagery (professional handshake, clean architectural glass, or abstract connectivity patterns).
*   **Message**: "Investor Grievance: Committed to Fair Resolution."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the grievance redressal mechanism and our commitment to investor protection.

### 3. Institutional Grievance Form (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-precision, clean form interface with real-time validation and a secure file upload zone for supporting documents.

### 4. Resolution Roadmap
*   **Visual Strategy**: A step-by-step industrial timeline (Submit → Review → Resolve) showing the grievance lifecycle and resolution windows.

### 5. Direct Support Hub (Contact)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density contact card featuring dedicated IR channels (Email, Phone, and Nodal Officer details).

### 6. Grievance Tracking Engine
*   **Visual Strategy**: A minimalist tracking portal where investors can input a Reference ID to see their real-time resolution status.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: A supportive assistance banner for immediate investor queries.
