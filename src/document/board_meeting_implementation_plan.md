# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Board Meeting Notices" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Board Meeting Notices" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Board Meeting Notices.md`. The design will focus on institutional transparency, governance clarity, and professional accessibility.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-end boardroom imagery (close-up of a polished conference table or a modern corporate glass facade).
*   **Message**: "Board Meeting Notices: Institutional Clarity & Strategic Governance."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist typography framing board notices as the cornerstone of executive accountability.

### 3. Notices Listing (Main)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A systematic, high-density listing of board meeting proclamations.
*   **Row Design**: Clear agenda highlights, date markers, and primary action triggers (View/Download).

### 4. Notice Detail View
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive modal featuring a technical PDF viewer optimized for executive proclamation text.

### 5. Archive / Filter
*   **Visual Strategy**: Advanced discovery bar allowing filters by fiscal year and quarterly cycles.

### 6. Governance Note
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical info-cards or a sidebar detailing board structure and institutional compliance practices.

### 7. CTA Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Professional banner with a "Connect with Governance Desk" primary action.
