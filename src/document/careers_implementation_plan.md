# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Careers Overview" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Careers Overview" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Careers Overview.md`. The design will focus on employer branding, professional growth, and talent acquisition efficiency.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-fidelity industrial team imagery (collaborative staff, precision manufacturing floor, or modern corporate office).
*   **Message**: "Build Your Legacy. Shape the Future of Precision Tubing."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Narrative-driven typography highlighting J pan's position as a growth-centric employer.

### 3. Value Propositions (Why Work With Us)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A grid of 4-6 high-authority cards using minimalist icons to communicate Career Growth, L&D, Stability, and Work Culture.

### 4. Culture Pulse (Life at Jpan)
*   **Visual Strategy**: An immersive, masonry-style image gallery showcasing team interactions, workplace aesthetics, and corporate events.

### 5. Opportunity Hub (Open Positions)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density grid of job cards featuring "Apply Now" triggers, location markers, and experience requirements.

### 6. Department Segments
*   **Visual Strategy**: A categorized navigation or filter system to group roles by Engineering, Manufacturing, HR, and Operations.

### 7. Institutional Benefits
*   **Visual Strategy**: A clean list or horizontal scroller highlighting competitive incentives, training programs, and work-life balance.

### 8. Onboarding Roadmap (Hiring Process)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A linear step-by-step progress visual (1. Apply → 2. Interview → 3. Selection) using technical iconography.
