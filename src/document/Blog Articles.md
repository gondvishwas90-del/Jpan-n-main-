Blog Articles Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Blog / Articles”
Short tagline (example: “Insights, updates, and industry knowledge”)
Background: industry / workspace image

2. Featured Article (Optional 🔥)
Highlight latest / important blog
Block:
Image
Title
Short summary (2–3 lines)
Date
“Read More” CTA

3. Blog Listing (Main)
Grid layout (2–3 columns)
Each Article Card:
Featured image
Title
Short description (2 lines)
Date
“Read More” button

4. Categories / Filters (Important)
Categories (tabs or sidebar):
Industry Insights
Company Updates
Technology
Search bar

5. Article Detail Page (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Date
Author (optional)
Featured image
Full content (headings + paragraphs)
Images inside content
Share buttons

6. Related Articles
2–3 article cards
“You may also like”

7. CTA Section
Line:
“Stay updated with our latest insights”
Button:
Contact Us / Subscribe

8. Footer
Same global footer
