Investor Presentation Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Investor Presentation”
Short tagline (example: “Insights into our business performance and growth strategy”)
Background: corporate / analytics image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
Purpose of presentations
Company overview for investors

3. Presentations Listing (Main 🔥)
Grid / list layout
Each Card:
Presentation title (example: “Investor Presentation – Q2 2025”)
Date
Short 1–2 line summary
“View” button
“Download PDF” button

4. Presentation Detail View (On Click)
Inside:
Title
Date
Short overview (optional)
Embedded PDF / slides viewer
Download option

5. Key Highlights (Optional Premium)
Small section showing:
Revenue growth
Market presence
Expansion plans
Icons + short text

6. Archive / Filter
Filter by:
Year
Quarter
Search option

7. Investor Communication CTA
Line:
“Explore our growth and performance in detail”
Button:
Contact Investor Relations / Contact Us

8. Footer
Same global footer
