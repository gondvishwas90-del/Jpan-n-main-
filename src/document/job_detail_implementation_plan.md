# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Job Detail" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Job Detail" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Job Detail.md`. The design will focus on role clarity, technical precision, and conversion efficiency.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Immersive workplace background (staff in action or modern industrial setting).
*   **Message**: Job Title (e.g., "Senior Production Engineer") with location and experience markers.

### 2. Job Summary
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical narrative overview outlining the department and reporting hierarchy.

### 3. Role Blueprint (Key Responsibilities)
*   **Visual Strategy**: High-precision bulleted list defining daily tasks, role duties, and team collaboration requirements.

### 4. Competency Framework (Skills & Qualifications)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A structured grid or list focusing on Education, Technical Proficiency, and specialized Certifications.

### 5. Job Specification Block
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density info card featuring Type, Location, Experience, and fiscal markers (Salary).

### 6. Institutional Perks (Benefits)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A clean module highlighting the professional and personal value provided to the candidate.

### 7. Application Engine (Apply Now Form)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-fidelity, secure form for candidate data capture and resume upload.

### 8. Talent Discovery (Related Jobs)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A horizontal grid of job cards to encourage further discovery within the J pan talent ecosystem.
