# Implementation Plan: Jpan Tubular "Credit Rating" Page

This document outlines the systematic approach to building the "Credit Rating" page for J pan Tubular, based on the content structure provided in `Credit Rating.md`. The design will prioritize financial authority, historical transparency, and stakeholder trust.

---

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist financial-industrial imagery (bullion textures, architectural glass, or clean fiscal charts).
*   **Message**: "Credit Rating: Independent Validation of Financial Strength."

### 2. Intro Section
*   **Visual Strategy**: Technical typography explaining the significance of creditworthiness and institutional evaluation.

### 3. Institutional Alpha (Current Rating Highlight)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-visibility, "Golden-Ratio" banner featuring the current credit rating, agency branding, and outlook status.

### 4. Fiscal Archive (Rating History)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A high-density table or grid layout featuring chronological ratings, agency outlooks, and direct report triggers.

### 5. Agency Profiles
*   **Visual Strategy**: Minimalist logo grid for rating agencies (ICRA, CRISIL, CARE, etc.) with brief institutional context.

### 6. Immersive Report Hub (Detail Viewer)
*   **Visual Strategy**: A full-screen reading experience with an integrated PDF viewer for detailed rating rationale reports.

### 7. Stability Framework
*   **Visual Strategy**: A narrative section using high-fidelity icons to communicate company stability, growth performance, and risk management.
