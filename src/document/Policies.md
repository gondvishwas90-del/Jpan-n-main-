Policies Page Structure
1. Hero Section
Page title: “Policies”
Short tagline (example: “Our corporate policies and governance framework”)
Background: corporate / legal / office image

2. Intro Section
3–4 lines:
Importance of company policies
Commitment to transparency & ethics

3. Policies Listing (Main 🔥)
Grid / list layout
Each Policy Card:
Policy name (example: “Code of Conduct Policy”)
Short 1-line description
“View” button
“Download PDF” button

4. Policy Detail View (On Click)
Inside:
Policy title
Short overview (optional)
Full document (embedded PDF)
Download option

5. Categories / Filter (Important)
Filter by:
Governance Policies
HR Policies
Compliance Policies
Search option

6. Key Policies Highlight (Optional)
3–4 important policies featured
Short explanation

7. CTA Section
Line:
“For more information on our policies, contact us”
Button:
Contact Us

8. Footer
Same global footer
